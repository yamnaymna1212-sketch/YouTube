# Dollarly — the user's rules (non-negotiable)

## Look (brand v5, `style/BRAND-v5-dollarly.md`)
- **Mix:** ≈ 70% explainer in the paper style (stage with White House/Capitol on Pennsylvania Avenue, paper maps, pawns + route arcs, House/Senate seat charts, quote cards, doors, calendars, night map with glowing outline) · ≈ 20% **Jake living the story** (his apartment, TV, phone, bills, cousin Dana) · **≤ 10% real material**, used as a moment (on Jake's TV, a framed print), never a slideshow.
- **Rich world, not icons on empty paper:** every explainer shot uses `World` (Washington skyline, floor, small pawns walking, Jake reacting at the side). "Keep it like the hook."
- **No dead second:** something new every 1–1.5 s (badge pops/swaps, pawn hops, route draws, seats flip, camera move, real clip, Jake reacts). A still image never sits alone. One icon slot that swaps on the words beats four labels.
- **Minimal text:** ≤ 5 words on screen at once (+ the small source chip). Prefer an icon to a word. Big type for TV.
- **Pro motion:** springs, eased camera, motion that carries into the next shot (fly into a door/TV/pin, slide, rise). Camera drift must settle on long shots (never runaway zoom).
- **Linked:** story → dive into TV/phone → explainer → back to Jake. Every scene hands a shape, motion or question to the next.
- Source chip on every shot; Dollarly bug bottom-right; film grain.

## Truth & safety
- All material real and licensed: U.S. government works (White House portraits, NASA, National Archives, House), Prelinger Archives (PD), uscurrency.gov SPECIMEN notes, U.S. Census / Natural Earth maps. Credit on screen + `LICENSES.md`.
- **No AI-generated or AI-altered faces. No company logos as standalone images. No news-channel footage.**
- Illustrative characters labelled as illustrative. Hypotheticals labelled WHAT IF. Neutral politics: both sides, no prediction, no named politician's motives.
- Not financial advice (said once, on screen once).

## Sound
- Calm dark ambient music only; SFX tick/type/counter/paper ≤ 0.1 (one real alarm only if the user asks). **No hiss beds.**
- VO: every pause cut clean, VO louder than the music; final mix −14 LUFS.

## Workflow
- Save and push after every step. Arabic with the user. Give real ETAs. Render on the user's Mac M3 when possible.
