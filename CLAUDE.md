## Channel
- Emotional animated short films for a US / English audience (references: Day One Films "DOPAMINE", Monk Mode Films).
- Signature: TODAY (daily spend) + BANK (balance) HUD in the corner; red devil (bad inner voice) and green angel (good advice), both clearly visible with clear voices. The devil grows and the angel shrinks with bad choices.
- Widescreen 16:9 YouTube videos (not Shorts), cinematic lighting with no white blowout and no over-saturation, realistic US places, varied weather and time of day.

## Voice
- NEVER use robotic TTS (espeak / mbrola / festival). Use natural voices (Kokoro-82M from npm `kokoro-q8-shards` + `kokoro-js` voices).
- Main character: a young, energetic voice with a slightly faster pace (e.g. `am_puck` / `am_adam`, speed ~1.1–1.15), not slow or flat.

## Feedback after DELIVERED. (Oct 2026), apply to every next video
1. **Ambient music** under the whole film (soft pads/piano/texture that follows the mood), not just short stingers.
2. **Calm sound effects**: soft and natural, never loud or harsh.
3. **Don't stay too long on one shot**: cut more often (about 2–4 s per shot) so the viewer never gets bored.
4. **The character moves a lot**: gestures, body motion, head turns, breathing, walking.
5. **Many facial expressions**: change them often and match them to the moment.
6. **Many varied camera angles**: wide, medium, close-up, over-the-shoulder, low and high angles, push-ins and pans.
7. **Main voice**: young and fast (see Voice above).
