# Channel Rules

Read this before any work on this repo. The owner talks in Arabic: reply in Arabic. Video content is in English for a US audience.

## Format
1. **Shorts first.** Every video is vertical 9:16 (1080x1920) for TikTok and YouTube Shorts, split into "Part 1, Part 2...". **Episodes must be longer than 60 seconds (aim for 61-75 s)**: TikTok Creator Rewards only pays on original videos over 1 minute. Episode 1 (30 s) was a test cut and doesn't qualify.
2. Long 16:9 videos come later, only after Shorts show real views and engagement. Don't plan them yet.
3. Keep text inside the Shorts safe zone: nothing important in the top 220 px, the right 150 px, or the bottom 300 px.

## Style
4. **Avoid saturated styles.** The neon-noir, lo-fi room and 3D-diorama looks are everywhere. `style/neon-noir-style-bible.md` and `brand/0*.png` are archived (v1) and no longer used.
5. **Current style: "Glow" characters in a "Blueprint Neon" world** (`style/glow-style-bible.md`): glowing stick characters with faces, in a navy blueprint city with a square grid and one neon color per place. Never a plain black background.
6. **Main character: Kai**, a blue glowing stickman with flowing water-like hair, like Watergirl from Fireboy & Watergirl. The other characters use the same skeleton with different colors, head shapes and hair.
7. **The glow is the story:** it shows energy, money and sleep. Bills drain it, sleep and wins refill it.

## Niche (locked)
**Money stories.** "Animated stories about the money moments Americans live through, and how Kai survives them." Start in this niche from day one: don't do general content first, because the audience you build would be the wrong one.
- **Story first, never a lesson.** No explainers, no advice, no "tips", no claims or statistics. The viewer feels the money moment through a character. The money is the hidden problem in every story.
- Mix: ~60% drama with money inside (exes, roommates, family, landlord, scams), ~30% stories where a money mistake or win is the plot, ~10% audience-participation endings.
- Post every episode to TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Reels. Check each platform's monetization eligibility for the owner's country before assuming it applies.

## Content
8. Real everyday US problems: money, rent, college debt, gas prices, sleep, jobs. Target people living them, ages 18-30.
9. Use real American details (flag, dollars, burgers, gas prices) but **never real brand logos**.
10. Hook in the first 2 seconds, cliffhanger at the end of each part, and one practical tip that's actually true.
