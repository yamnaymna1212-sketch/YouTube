# Channel Rules

Read this before any work on this repo. The owner talks in Arabic: reply in Arabic. Video content is in English for a US audience.

## Format
1. **Shorts first.** Every video is vertical 9:16 (1080x1920) for TikTok and YouTube Shorts, split into "Part 1, Part 2...".
2. **Length: 30, 35 or 40 seconds. Never more than 40.** (Owner's rule.) Cut lines before you let an episode run over. Trade-off the owner accepted: TikTok Creator Rewards only pays on videos over 1 minute, so these short episodes build followers and views toward eligibility but won't earn there; YouTube Shorts has no length condition on its payouts.
3. Long 16:9 videos come later, only after Shorts show real views and engagement. Don't plan them yet.
4. Keep text inside the Shorts safe zone: nothing important in the top 220 px, the right 150 px, or the bottom 300 px.

## Style
5. **Avoid saturated styles.** The neon-noir, lo-fi room and 3D-diorama looks are everywhere. `style/neon-noir-style-bible.md` and `brand/0*.png` are archived (v1) and no longer used.
6. **Current style: "Glow" characters in a "Blueprint Neon" world** (`style/glow-style-bible.md`): glowing stick characters with faces, in a navy blueprint city with a square grid and one neon color per place. Never a plain black background.
7. **Main character: Kai**, a blue glowing stickman with flowing water-like hair, like Watergirl from Fireboy & Watergirl. The other characters use the same skeleton with different colors, head shapes and hair.
8. **The glow is the story:** it shows energy, money and sleep. Bills drain it, sleep and wins refill it.

## Niche (locked)
**Money stories.** "Animated stories about the money moments Americans live through, and how Kai survives them." Start in this niche from day one: don't do general content first, because the audience you build would be the wrong one.
- **Story first, never a lesson.** No explainers, no advice, no "tips", no claims or statistics. The viewer feels the money moment through a character. The money is the hidden problem in every story.
- Mix: ~60% drama with money inside (exes, roommates, family, landlord, scams), ~30% stories where a money mistake or win is the plot, ~10% audience-participation endings.
- Post every episode to TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Reels. Check each platform's monetization eligibility for the owner's country before assuming it applies.

## Content
9. Real everyday US problems: money, rent, college debt, gas prices, sleep, jobs. Target people living them, ages 18-30.
10. Use real American details (flag, dollars, burgers, gas prices) but **never real brand logos**.
11. **Hook (first 3 seconds): a clear contradiction plus a clear problem, so the viewer asks the question in their own head.** Never put "how" or "why" in the hook, and never ask the question out loud. Show two things that clash, e.g. "I ate a $14 salad. I paid $51.50." The viewer thinks "wait, what?" and stays.
12. Cliffhanger at the end of each part, then a short follow/share line with a joke about the story. Don't add a practical tip unless the episode has room and it's actually true.
13. **After every finished video, always give the owner its title and the recommended TikTok caption + hashtags** (4 fixed + 1-2 rotating by topic, from `brand/tiktok/hashtags.md`). Do this in the same message that delivers the video, and save them in the episode's `script.md`.
