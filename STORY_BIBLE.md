# Story Bible: "Kai | Money Stories"

Animated Shorts about the money moments Americans live through. The money is the hidden problem; the story is the feeling. Story first, never a lesson.

## Cast (look: `style/glow-style-bible.md`, `brand/v2-glow/02-cast.png`)
| Character | Who | Look |
|-----------|-----|------|
| **Kai** | 22, delivery driver + community-college student, Ohio. Calm, tries to do the right thing, can't say no to people he cares about | Blue glow, flowing water-like hair |
| **Brooke** | Kai's ex. Always has a reason, always needs something ("pay u back friday") | Pink glow, long hair |
| **Mint** | Kai's roommate. Broke but calm | Green glow, beanie |
| **Rosa** | Coworker, always in a rush | Pink-red glow, flame hair |
| **Mr. Gold** | Landlord, steady glow that never dims | Amber glow, square head, tie |

Home: apartment **214** (Kai + Mint, rent $1,150/month). Kai's balance in the story so far: **$39.12**. Kai's glow = his energy; bills drain it, sleep and wins refill it.
Places on his street, in order: Home, Gas station (gas $3.89), 24/7 Market, Burgers ($8.99 combo), Bank.

## Series so far

### Part 1: "The 5 AM Call" (30.6 s, posted)
5 AM, Kai's ex Brooke calls crying: "I miss us, can we start over?" He almost says yes, then says "No" and throws the phone; she calls again. Flashback in pink: two years, $6,400, "rent help" requests paid 14 times, balance $4,212 down to $39. He goes to block her; she texts "I'm outside." Knocks.

### Part 2: "The Door" (31.2 s, posted)
Hook: "At 5 AM, the door bangs." Three hard knocks. Through the peephole: Brooke with a suitcase, crying: "They locked me out. I have nowhere to go. I'll pay you back, I promise." Kai: fourteen times he'd heard those exact words ($300 x 14 = $4,200). He has $39.12; his hand is on the chain. A paper slides under the door: **FINAL NOTICE, Kai Walker, $2,300 past due.**

### Open threads
- Brooke has been using Kai's name (a card or account), and the $2,300 notice is addressed to him.
- Kai hasn't opened the door.
- Mint (roommate) is asleep behind Kai; Mr. Gold (landlord) hasn't appeared yet (a 5 AM racket on a door is the kind of thing a landlord notices).
- Brooke's real situation (locked out because of what? whose lease?) is unrevealed.

## Story 2: "Rent Day" (new arc, the ex arc is closed)
The owner stopped the Brooke storyline after Part 2: no more ex episodes. Part 3 ("In My Name", `episodes/ep01-part3-in-my-name`) was built but will not be posted.
- **Part 1: "Rent Day"** (34.3 s, rendered, `episodes/ep02-rent-day-part1`): rent is due Thursday, payday is Friday. Kai searches pockets, jacket, couch: $8.37. Mr. Gold knocks: "Just a friendly reminder. Tomorrow." Kai: "Yes sir! Tomorrow!" 11:58 PM, Mint walks in with an envelope marked RENT: "Hey... can we talk about my half of the rent?"
- **Owner's direction for this arc:** real everyday US problems (rent, gas, paycheck), small amounts only, no claims of gains or losses, no advice, no sensitive money words (debt, loan, fees, invest).
- **Editing rule (owner):** many short shots (about 1-3 s), new places and camera angles, and every cut must be motivated by the story (a sound, an object, a reaction, time passing), never random. See the shot table in `episodes/ep02-rent-day-part1/script.md`.

## Voice & tone
English, US audience 18-30, plain first-person past tense, short sentences, no jargon, no statistics, no advice. The narrator is Kai. Humor lives in the follow/share line at the end.
