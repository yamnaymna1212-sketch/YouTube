# Episode 1: The 5 AM Call (Part 1)

**Length:** 30 seconds · **Format:** 9:16 · **Style:** Glow characters in the Blueprint Neon world
**Cast:** Kai (blue, 22) and Brooke (pink, his ex)
**Storyboard:** `storyboard.png`, frames `frame-01.png` to `frame-10.png`

## Changes to the original idea
1. **Hook "At 5 AM in America" → "5:00 AM."** Americans don't say "in America" about their own lives; it sounds like a foreign narrator. An exact time on a red alarm clock feels real and lands in under a second.
2. **Caller ID "Brooke (ex) 🚩".** Saving an ex with a red-flag emoji is a real Gen Z habit, and US viewers read it instantly.
3. **New ending: "I'm outside."** After the flashback he decides to block her, and the text arrives first, with pink light under his door. This is the cliffhanger for Part 2.

## Hook (0:00-0:05), rendered: `hook.mp4`
- **Title card (0:00-0:03):** "AT 5 AM, MY PHONE RANG..." in a white box, on screen from the first frame
- **Narrator** (starts at 0:00, clear, fast, no pauses, `audio/hook-vo-andrew.mp3`): "At 5 AM, my phone rang. It's my ex, and she's crying."
- **Mix:** effects sit about 12 dB under the voice and duck further while he speaks
- **Sound:** urgent digital ring + phone vibrating on the nightstand from frame 1, whoosh on the zoom, bass hit when "ex" lands, click when he answers, heartbeat after
- **Zoom on the main element only:** the phone lifts off the nightstand and fills the screen; the room stays in place, dims and blurs
- **Captions:** word by word in sync with the voice; key words in color (5:00 AM red, EX pink, CRYING blue)
- All sounds are original (no copyrighted ringtones)

## Acting notes
- **Kai:** slow and sleepy at first, small movements. Pause before "No." The throw is sudden, then he freezes, shocked at himself.
- **Brooke:** real crying, broken breath, voice cracks on "start over". During the wait, a tiny hopeful smile through tears.
- **Glow as emotion:** Kai dim when asleep → warm pink flicker while tempted → bright and sharp on "No." → shaky during the flashback.
