# EP03 — "What Happens to the World If the U.S. Nuked Iran?" (working title)

Alt titles: "One Order, Six Rings: What a Nuclear Strike on Iran Would Do to the World" · "The Day After: Iran, America, and Your Wallet".
Format ≈ 10 min, English, hypothetical + explanatory (not advocacy). Style: `style/STYLE-v4-paperworld.md` — **signature device: the Ring Zoom**.

## What we took from the reference script (`presidential_roads_script.pdf`) — as principles, not text
| Technique in the reference | How we use it (our own version) |
|---|---|
| Opens with a quote + a historical echo, then an open loop ("this president walked all three") | Cold open = one calm sentence + the promise "six rings, and the last one is not what you expect" |
| Rule of three (three keys, three roads, three things to watch) | Fixed frame, but ours is **six rings** that widen with distance and time |
| Each case ends on a punchy outcome line with an exact number ("35 to 19, one vote short") | Every ring ends on one hard number + the Jake wallet meter |
| Mid-video promise "we'll come back to him" | Planted at 0:40 and paid off at Ring 6 |
| Human oddities (printed tickets, "Spotty Lincoln") | One surprising real detail per ring, from a primary source |
| Callback ending + comment question | Callback to the opening sentence; "Which ring surprised you?" |
**Avoided on purpose:** its date-dense wall of names (we add stakes and scale instead), its text/cases verbatim, its pawn-stage look copied (we use the Ring Zoom map instead).

## The through-line character
- **Jake** (Dollarly) is the viewer's stand-in, 7:42 a.m. at the pump when the news breaks. He reacts to every ring.
- In each ring a small **paper citizen** lives the moment (driver, shopkeeper, farmer, fisherman, a dog on a roof): flat, dignified, non-graphic, never a real person's face.

## Structure (where to start: with ONE person's first minute, then zoom out — best for retention)
0. **Cold open (0:00–0:45):** Jake at the pump, $4.15. "Nothing has happened. Yet. This is a what-if — here is exactly what the science, the economy and the Constitution say." Open loop: "The thing that could stop it is in Ring 6."
1. **Ring 0 — the first second:** what one weapon does, drawn as growing paper circles on a real map (✔ source: Glasstone & Dolan, *Effects of Nuclear Weapons*; Wellerstein NUKEMAP ⚠️). Underground vs. surface burst matters: Iran's key sites are buried ⚠️ (Fordow/Natanz). Myth-bust: "Do all cars die? (EMP)" ⚠️.
2. **Ring 1 — the first hour, the nearest city:** cars, power, hospitals, animals, water. Told through 3 paper citizens. No gore; consequences shown as lights going out and icons.
3. **Ring 2 — the first week, the region:** fallout wind, desalination plants & Gulf water ⚠️, airlines, refugees, the Strait of Hormuz (≈20% of world oil ⚠️ EIA).
4. **Ring 3 — the first month, the money:** oil, gas pump, stocks, rates, your paycheck. Anchors: 1973, 1990, 2022 shocks ⚠️. Ranges, not predictions.
5. **Ring 4 — the world:** the 80-year taboo (last use 1945), allies, Russia/China, the NPT, who else decides to build one ⚠️.
6. **Ring 5 — America, and the one thing that can stop it:** who can order it (CRS: sole presidential authority ⚠️), what Congress still holds — the **three keys: money, oversight, war** — with real precedents (1973 Cambodia funding cutoff; impeachment trials 1868/1999 ⚠️). Link to the current 2026 context from the user's script ⚠️ verify each claim.
7. **Close:** callback to the opening line, Jake's final wallet number, "Which ring surprised you?".

## Retention mechanics
- A visible **ring counter** (Ring 0→5 + distance/time stamp) so the viewer always knows where we are and what's coming.
- Pattern interrupt every 25–40 s: new shot type, new citizen, or a Jake reaction. Chapter cards double as YouTube chapters.
- "What we know / what we don't" labels (high/medium/low confidence) → credibility, and it fits the Dollarly "Decoded" promise.
- Soft SFX only (≤ 0.1), dark ambient music 0.07, narration pauses tightened (existing pipeline).

## Safety / platform notes
Hypothetical stated in the first 15 s and in the description; no graphic imagery; no real-person faces drawn or AI-generated; every number sourced; no operational/targeting detail. ⚠️ items must be resolved in `research.md` before scripting.
