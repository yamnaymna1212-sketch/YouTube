# EP03 — Hybrid shot grammar (story + real material, one coherent look)

User direction (6 Nov 2026): NOT all cartoon. A cartoon-only video feels disconnected from the real topic and viewers leave. Mix the Jake story with **real footage, real photos, real statements and plausible-scenario explanation**, with constant variety, and make it match the thumbnail promise.

## Rotation (no shot type lasts > 8 s; never two identical types in a row)
| Type | Share | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| A. **Jake story** (cartoon, always alive) | ~30% | 4:00 AM → alert → bunker → screens. LiveJake never frozen: breathing, blinks, saccades, spring reactions |
| B. **Paper World explainer** | ~25% | Ring Zoom map, pawns/citizens, label cards, wallet meter |
| C. **Real archival footage / photos** | ~25% | public-domain only (see sources), graded into our palette, shown as taped prints or full-bleed with grain |
| D. **Statement & document cards** | ~12% | exact quotes with source chip; screenshots of official pages (CRS, congress.gov, EIA, IAEA) |
| E. **Maps & data** | ~8% | NASA Earth imagery of Iran/Gulf/Hormuz, EIA charts, animated counters |

## Making real + cartoon feel like one film
- Same **frame language**: cream paper border, thin ink outline, hard shadow; real photos sit as taped prints (slight rotation) or full-bleed with the same grain + vignette.
- Same **colour grade**: real footage pulled toward warm desaturation, red/amber accents only.
- Same **motion**: slow push-ins + parallax on every still; Jake and citizens react to the real material (he leans in, flinches, reads the document).
- **Cut on meaning**: every real clip is a proof of the sentence being spoken, never filler.

## Story ↔ proof loop (per chapter)
`Jake beat (A) → question → explainer (B) → real proof (C/D) → data (E) → back to Jake reacting (A)`

## Real-material sources to use (all ⚠️ verify licence before use)
- Official U.S. government photos/video (White House, DoD/DVIDS, DOE/NNSA, National Archives): public domain unless a third-party credit is shown.
- Historic nuclear-test films/photos: DOE/NNSA, National Archives, Prelinger (e.g. 1950s civil-defence films).
- NASA Earth observation: Iran, Persian Gulf, Strait of Hormuz.
- Documents: CRS reports, congress.gov (War Powers Resolution), EIA charts, IAEA reports, FCC report on the 2018 Hawaii false missile alert.
- **Not allowed:** news-channel clips, stock photos of unknown licence, AI-generated or AI-altered faces, company logos as standalone images.
- Environment note: Wikimedia returns 429 to this server → use the original agency sites / archive.org, or have the user upload files to `studio/public/iran/`.

## Statements
Quote only verifiable statements (official transcripts, Federal Register, whitehouse.gov remarks), word-for-word, with date + source chip. No paraphrase presented as a quote.
