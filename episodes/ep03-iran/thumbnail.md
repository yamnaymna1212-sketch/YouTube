# EP03 thumbnail — spec (made by the user after the video is finished)

Reference look (user-provided example): desaturated real portrait of the leader on the left + a high-contrast map filled in red + 2-word bold white-on-black label.
Our version: **Iran map + a real nuclear-test photo + the leader portrait**, all real, label e.g. `WHAT IF?` / `ONE ORDER`.

Rules
- **Promise = content.** The video must show, in its first 30 s, the decision, the map and a real statement, so the thumbnail is honest.
- Include **"WHAT IF"** (or equivalent) on the thumbnail and in the title/description: it depicts a hypothetical, not an event that happened.
- Portrait: official U.S. government photo (public domain) only — verify the specific file's licence. No AI-generated or AI-altered face. Plain grayscale/colour grade is fine.
- Bomb image: a real archival test photograph (DOE/NNSA/National Archives). Map: NASA or public-domain base map.
- ⚠️ YouTube "altered or synthetic content" disclosure: a photo-composite showing a real person with a bomb may need the label or a clearly hypothetical caption. Re-check the current policy text before upload; keep the "WHAT IF" label regardless.
- Must read at 120 px wide: max 3 elements, 2 words, text ≥ 18% of frame height.
