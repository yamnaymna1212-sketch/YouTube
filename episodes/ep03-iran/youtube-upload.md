It's 4 a.m. A siren. Jake, 26, doesn't know what's happening, and neither does anyone on his street. This is a what-if, built on a real war, real laws and real numbers: if a U.S. president ordered a nuclear strike on Iran, what would happen in the first second, the first hour, the first week and the first month? We follow it ring by ring, from a town near the blast to the Strait of Hormuz, to the gas pump, to Congress. And the last ring is the one that could stop it.

⚠️ Hypothetical scenario. No nuclear weapon has been used and this is not a prediction. The U.S.–Iran war since February 2026 is real; the nuclear order is not.
