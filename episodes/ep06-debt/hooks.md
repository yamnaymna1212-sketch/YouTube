## Patterns
1. **A per-second / per-day number inside the first 12 s** (PENNY $500/s, Infographics $3B/day). The biggest recent winner puts the money in second 0.
2. **Almost every title says collapse / crisis / breaking / imploded.** Red ocean. Nobody leads with the household cost (mortgage payment, per-person share) or with the honest "it's not collapsing, here's what it does cost you".
3. Personal translation ($2,800–3,000 per American, $55/mo per quarter-point, "household owes $297K") appears in the winners but late (3–5 min). We can open with it.
4. Our verified numbers match theirs where they overlap (interest ≈ $3B/day, ≈ $117–119K per person, $2.1T by 2036); their "$1.25T interest" is NOT Treasury net interest (MTS = $1.052T): do not copy it.
