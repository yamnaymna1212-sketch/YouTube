## Do not say / not used
- No number for how far stocks, oil or gas would move in this what-if. Only yardsticks.
- No casualty counts, blast radius, targets, weapon type, or who used it. "Somewhere in the Gulf" only.
- No claim that markets would close for days or that banks would fail. The VO says what the rules are and what happened before.
- No name of any politician. Neutral.
