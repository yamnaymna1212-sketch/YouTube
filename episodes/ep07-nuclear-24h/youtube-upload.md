## Description
A what-if, hour by hour. In a port city on the Gulf, a nuclear weapon is used. Sam, a delivery driver, has minutes to find shelter. Jake, thousands of miles away, has a phone, a TV and a gas tank. What does the official guidance say? And what happens to stocks, oil, gas and your bank in the first day?

This is a hypothetical scenario for education. It is not safety instructions and not financial advice. In a real emergency, follow your local officials. Characters are illustrative. Archival film is a 1946 U.S. test (Operation Crossroads), shown for context, not the scenario.
