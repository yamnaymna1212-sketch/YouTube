# STYLE v4 — "Paper World" × Dollarly (for explainers of power and events)

Goal: the reference channel's *visual grammar* (flat paper-craft diagram world, pawn characters, label cards, chapter markers) fused with Dollarly's own identity (Jake, the wallet meter, real documents, "Your Money. Decoded.").
We copy the **grammar**, never their assets, characters, layouts frame-by-frame or name. Everything is drawn by us in code.

## 1. The look
- **Ground:** warm cream paper `#EDE6D3` with faint paper fibres + soft vignette (the paper IS the stage; never pure white or black).
- **Line:** thick ink outline `#1B2230`, flat fills, hard offset shadow (4 px, 18% ink). No gradients except paper vignette.
- **Palette = Dollarly's, warmed:** Ink `#1B2230` · Paper `#EDE6D3` · Bone `#F6F1E4` · Steel-blue `#3F5A8C` (one side) · Brick red `#B4452F` (the other side / danger) · Amber `#D9A441` (money ONLY) · Pine `#4E7A4A` (map markers).
- **Type:** condensed bold sans for labels (Barlow Condensed / Oswald class, caps), Instrument Serif for big numbers and years, JetBrains Mono for sources. Labels sit in boxed "cards" with a hard shadow.
- **Camera:** locked wide stage + slow push-ins, parallax on the paper. Cuts every 3–6 s. Always something moving.

## 2. The cast (all drawn in code, no real faces, no AI faces)
| Who | Drawn as | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Institutions | flat buildings: White House, Capitol, Pentagon, Tehran skyline (generic), oil terminal | label on top in a card |
| Leaders / countries | **pawns** with a coloured base (blue = US, red = Iran, grey = others) and a role label ("THE PRESIDENT", "TEHRAN") | NEVER a caricature of a real person; the pawn carries the role, not the face |
| Advisers / generals / markets | small pawns with a prop (hat, phone, bull/bear card) | max 3 on stage |
| **Jake** (Dollarly) | our existing LiveJake, flat-coloured to this palette, standing at street level under the stage or walking along the bottom "Pennsylvania Avenue" strip | He is the audience's wallet: he reacts to every beat (surprise, worry, doubt, shrug) |
| Real evidence | photos / documents / screenshots as *taped paper prints* on the board (slight rotation, tape corners) | only public-domain / licensed, always with a source chip |

## 3. Making the characters live (the "live the moment" rule)
Every pawn is rigged, not static:
- **Idle life:** breathing squash (1.5%), slow sway, head-tilt noise, eyes/glasses glint, shadow pulses with the squash.
- **Spring reactions on cue:** hop (surprise), lean-in (suspicion), shrink + shake (fear), puff-up (pride), turn-away (refusal), bow (deference). Springs only (damping 12–18), no linear moves, no random jitter.
- **Locomotion:** pawns *slide-hop* along the road strip with a squash on each landing; Jake keeps his walk cycle.
- **Interaction:** objects react to pawns (a document pawn "reads", a phone buzzes, a button gets pressed, the map pins pop in with a bounce).
- **Consequence animation:** every claim is *shown*: oil barrel icons flow through a narrow strait; a gas-pump counter spins; Jake's WALLET meter ticks up/down.

## 4. The Dollarly mix (what stays ours)
- Opening 8 s: Jake + the wallet meter + the real number on screen, exactly like EP02.
- Each chapter ends on **"What it does to Jake's wallet"** (meter update).
- Real material is still the proof: official charts, EIA/NASA/National Archives images as taped prints.
- Channel end card, soft SFX only (tick/type/counter/paper, ≤ 0.1), dark ambient music at 0.07.
- Chapter pill in the player ("The Advice to Resign") → we use chapter cards + YouTube chapters.

## 5. Safety rules for geopolitical/violent topics
- Hypothetical framing stated in the first 15 s and in the description. Not advocacy for or against.
- No gore, no casualty "animation", no mushroom-cloud gag shots; consequences shown as maps, numbers and institutions.
- No fake quotes. Every statement attributed with a source chip; ⚠️ items re-checked on publish day.
- No logos of companies as standalone images; no deepfakes; no copied footage from news channels.
