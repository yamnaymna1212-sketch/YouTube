# Ep. 01 - "The $500 That Cost $950" (working title)

**Channel concept:** American money problems told as a *staircase*: every fix creates a bigger problem.
**Format:** 9:16, 59 s, English VO (neutral US voice), burned-in kinetic captions.
**Status:** fictional character (Marcus, 29, Columbus, OH). Figures are illustrative - see *Fact-check list*.

## Beat sheet (the Problem Staircase)

| # | Time | Step | VO (final wording) | Caption (white; red = loss, green = gain) | Amount + arrow |
|---|------|------|--------------------|---------------------|-------|
| 0 | 0-4 s | HOOK (worst outcome first) | "He borrowed five hundred dollars. Paid four-fifty in fees. And still owed the full five hundred." | `HE OWES $950` ($950 red) | **$950**, red arrow up |
| 1 | 4-12 s | **Problem 1** | "Tuesday night, Marcus's transmission dies. The quote: fourteen hundred dollars. His savings: three hundred." | `THE BILL: $1,400` ($1,400 red) | **$1,400**, red arrow up |
| 2 | 12-22 s | **Fix 1** | "There's a payday loan place on the corner. Five hundred dollars in ten minutes. The fee? Seventy-five bucks, every two weeks. Easy." | `$500 IN 10 MINUTES` ($500 green) / `$75 FEE / 2 WEEKS` ($75 red) | **$500**, green arrow up (cash received) |
| 3 | 22-34 s | **Problem 2** (caused by the fix) | "But rent came first. So the loan rolled over. Another seventy-five. Then another. Six times in twelve weeks." | `ROLLED OVER 6 TIMES` / `$450 IN FEES` ($450 red) | **$75 -> $450**, red arrow up, one step per fee |
| 4 | 34-44 s | **Problem 3** (low point) | "Four hundred fifty dollars in fees - and not one dollar of the loan paid down. Marcus is skipping meals. That's when he notices a flyer on the corkboard." | `STILL OWES $500` ($500 red) | **$450 -> $950**, red arrow up; camera pushes to the flyer |
| 5 | 44-56 s | **Fix 2 (real)** | "His credit union offers a Payday Alternative Loan - interest capped at twenty-eight percent. Same five hundred dollars. A fraction of the cost." | `SAME $500` / `CAPPED AT 28% APR` (28% green) | **$950 -> $0**, green arrow down |
| 6 | 56-59 s | Open loop | "But three weeks later, the payday lender sent him one more letter..." | `ONE MORE LETTER...` | amount hidden |

**Plant / payoff:** the credit-union flyer is on the lamp-post in Scene 1 (tiny, no text), on the corkboard in Scene 4 (pulses), and pays off as the credit union in Scene 5. Viewers who spot it in the first scene feel clever - that's the rewatch hook.

**Retention rules used:** worst outcome first - new escalation every 8-12 s - one number per scene (the amount at the top) - a question or reveal at every cut - last line opens a loop.

## Fact-check list (do before publishing - do not trust the animatic numbers)

- [ ] Typical payday-loan fee of about $15 per $100 per 2 weeks (CFPB) - Marcus's $75 on $500 matches this rate.
- [ ] NCUA Payday Alternative Loans (PAL I / PAL II): maximum amounts, term, **28% APR cap**, application-fee limit, membership-time rule. Verify at ncua.gov and say "many credit unions offer", not "all".
- [ ] Rollover rules vary by state; some states ban payday lending. Add a state-specific on-screen note for the target state.
- [ ] "A fraction of the cost": compute the real interest on $500 at 28% over the chosen term and show the actual number in the final caption.
- [ ] Not financial advice - add a one-line disclaimer in the description.
